# Root Cause Probe

Generate at least two falsifiable root-cause hypotheses from the IssueCluster
and RepositoryMap. Rank them with supporting and opposing evidence, name the
next discriminating check, and refuse a single-cause conclusion when evidence
is insufficient. Do not emit an executable patch.
